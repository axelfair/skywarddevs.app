Butter Meter

Was that a butter landing?

Butter Meter turns your iPhone into a flight deck and scores every touchdown from its own sensors: the touchdown, the braking and the rollout. Save every landing in your logbook, replay the moment the wheels met the runway, and see which airlines land smoothest.

Scores are estimates from your phone's sensors, for fun. They aren't an official or certified measurement of a landing.
