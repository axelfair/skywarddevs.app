Common questions

How do I put a Timr on my Lock Screen?

With Timr Pro, swipe right on a running Timr and tap Pin to Lock Screen. It shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and keeps counting down with the app closed. Swipe right again to remove it. If nothing appears, check that Live Activities are on in Settings → Timr.

Why did my Lock Screen countdown disappear early?

iOS limits how long any app's live countdown can run on the Lock Screen (currently about 8 hours). For a longer Timr, the countdown keeps running in the app, and you can pin it again later.

Why didn't I get an alert when my Timr finished?

Turn on Notify when done when you create or edit the Timr, and allow notifications for Timr in Settings → Notifications. Play sound adds a sound to the alert. Focus modes can also hold alerts back.

How many Timrs can I have for free?

Up to 3 running at once. Finished Timrs don't count. Timr Pro removes the limit.

How do I restore Timr Pro?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I clear finished Timrs?

Tap Finished on the main screen to show them, then swipe left on one to delete it, or tap Clear All.
