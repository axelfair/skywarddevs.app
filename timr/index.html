Timr

Watch time, not numbers.

Timr is a countdown timer for iPhone. Every Timr is a row of glass tubes filled with glowing colour that slowly drain as time passes, so you can see at a glance how much is left, from a 30-second egg timer to a holiday months away.

  • Quick timers from 15 seconds to 7 days, or count down to any date and time
  • Name each Timr and give it its own colour
  • End alerts, with or without sound
  • Timr Pro: unlimited Timrs, plus live countdowns on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island

Free for up to 3 active Timrs. Timr Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription.
