Something not working, advice that felt wrong, or an idea for HOLDr? Get in touch and include your device and the app version (Planning → Settings → Version). After a hold, the flight log from History → the hold → Send flight log helps a lot.

Common questions

How do I learn the app?

Run the guided demo. It starts on first launch, and later from Planning → Settings → Run guided demo. It takes about three minutes: you set up a hold, then watch a demo flight fly it on Live.

Which devices work?

An iPhone, or an iPad with cellular. Wi-Fi-only iPads have no GPS, so they can plan a hold but can't time it. Keep the device near a window, and on charge for long holds.

Why doesn't HOLDr give any advice before the fix?

Before the fix the aircraft is still slowing and the forecast wind may be wrong. HOLDr measures the real wind in the first turn and only then works out whether a different leg time is needed. Until then it shows the plan and an estimate for information.

When will it ask me to change the leg time?

At most once, and only when the hold is clearly heading outside the window. The new leg time is for the next lap. On the final lap it gives EXIT HOLD timing instead.

Where do the waypoints come from?

From you. HOLDr has no built-in database: create waypoints in the Waypoints tab, or open a list (CSV, text or PDF) that someone sends you. Every import is shown for checking before anything is saved. Check positions and holds against current charts.

How do I share waypoints or a hold?

Waypoints: in the Waypoints tab, tap ⋯ beside a set and choose Share. After a hold: Share on the summary, or open it in History.
