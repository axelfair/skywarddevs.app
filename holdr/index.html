HOLDr

Leave the hold on time.

HOLDr is a holding-pattern planner for airline pilots on iPad and iPhone. Enter the fix, your ETA, the Onwards (EFC) time, level, speed and wind, and it gives the number of laps and the FMC leg time. In the hold it follows your position from the device's GPS, measures the real wind in the first turn, and speaks up only if something needs to change.

  • Laps and FMC leg time to cross the fix 0 to 30 seconds before Onwards
  • Wind measured from your own track in the first turn: no forecast guesswork
  • One leg change at most, for the next lap, and EXIT HOLD timing on the final lap
  • A timing strip that shows where you'll cross the fix at a glance
  • Your own waypoint sets: create them, import a list, share them with your crew

Research software in beta testing. Not approved avionics and not for operational use: fly the hold by your normal procedures.
