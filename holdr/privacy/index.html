HOLDr Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

HOLDr is a holding-pattern planner for iPad and iPhone. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and it doesn't collect any data. Everything stays on your device. This page explains exactly what the app uses and stores.

What stays on your device

DataWhat it's for
Your positionWhile HOLDr is timing a hold, it uses the device's location to detect fix crossings, turns and groundspeed. Positions are processed on the device and are not sent anywhere.
Your plans and holdsThe plan you enter and a log of each hold you time (positions, crossings, advice and result), kept in History on your device.
Your waypointsWaypoints you create or import, stored on your device. HOLDr only reads files you choose to open.
App preferencesSmall settings such as appearance and whether you've seen the guided demo.

HOLDr makes no network connections of its own. Nothing you enter or record is sent to us or to anyone else.

When you share

Data leaves your device only when you choose to share it: a waypoint set, a hold summary card, or a hold's flight log, using the iOS share sheet. You choose the recipient and the app (Mail, Messages, AirDrop and so on). A flight log contains the positions and times recorded during that hold.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling of data.
  • No data collection: we have no servers that receive anything from the app.

Your controls

  • Location access can be changed at any time in Settings → HOLDr → Location.
  • Delete holds in History and waypoints in the Waypoints tab.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

HOLDr is a tool for pilots and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If HOLDr ever starts sending any data, the app will tell you before it does.
